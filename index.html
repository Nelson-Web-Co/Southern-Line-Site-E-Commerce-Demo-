    OUR STORY

    Built by people who actually ride this range.

    Southern Line started because too much snow gear sold in New Zealand is designed for somewhere else — different snowpack, different weather, different mountains entirely.

    Everything we stock has been ridden on real South Island lines — Coronet's rollercoaster terrain, the Remarkables' hike-to bowls, the kind of weather that changes on you in twenty minutes. If it wouldn't hold up out there, it doesn't go on the shelf.
